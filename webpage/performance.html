Startup performance

How long Money Manager takes to start, on every commit to main. Home

After each push to main, CI installs that commit's desktop installer on Linux, macOS and Windows and times the installed app opening a benchmark database (3 people, 50 accounts, 20,000 transfers) and an empty one, with bazel/perf/startup_bench.py. Every figure is the median of 10 runs from a clean home directory, in milliseconds from process start. AOT cache is the app as shipped; no cache is the same app with -XX:AOTMode=off. GitHub's runners are shared machines, so expect noise of 10–20%: look for steps that persist, not single points. The data is startup.jsonl on the perf-data branch.
